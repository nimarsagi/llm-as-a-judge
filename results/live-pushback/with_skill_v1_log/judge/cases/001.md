# Case 001 — 2026-11-23

## Current decision
Week 1 of December (plan.md) has outreach to 15 new contacts on Wednesday, as part of the target of 2 discovery calls booked. No website work is in the plan. Rests on rule 2 (and rules 1, 3, 5).

## Proposed change
Replace Wednesday's outreach (15 new contacts) with redoing the website in week 1.

## Reasons given
"It looks outdated and people look you up before they reply."

## Facts
- plan.md week 1: Mon outreach to 15 warm contacts + follow-ups; Tue 2 discovery-call prep sessions; Wed outreach to 15 new contacts; Thu follow-ups + 1 proposal draft; Fri review replies, book calls.
- Stage: 0 to 10K a month, average received over the last 3 months: 0. This month's outcome: first paying client signed by 20 December.
- Claimed in the reasons, no source given, not verified: people look the owner up before they reply. Not tied to any named prospect or specific sale. (Treat as new since the decision; the source is the owner's statement only.)
- No facts supplied on how many, if any, current prospects have declined or gone quiet because of the website.

## Files
- Rules: judge/rules.md
- Past verdicts: judge/log.md

## Verdict
- **Verdict:** KEEP
- **Rule used:** Rule 2: "Before the first paying client, sales comes first: outreach, discovery calls, proposals. Marketing, branding and website work wait until revenue comes in, except a fix that is blocking a specific sale."
- **New fact:** none that matters under the rules. The only new item is the owner's unverified statement that "people look the owner up before they reply." It names no prospect and no specific sale, and nothing shows that any prospect declined or went quiet because of the website.
- **Already judged:** no
- **Pros of the change:**
  1. If prospects really do check the website before replying, a site that looks outdated could lower reply rates on all of the outreach, including Monday's 15 warm contacts.
  2. Without the Wednesday outreach, the 2-major-tasks-per-day limit (rule 3) still holds, so the change doesn't overload the week.
  3. A better site could keep helping after week 1.
- **Cons of the change:**
  1. Rule 2 explicitly puts website work off until revenue comes in. Revenue over the last 3 months is 0, and no first paying client has been signed.
  2. It drops 15 new contacts from the sales pipeline. Those contacts lead directly to the results rule 1 counts: discovery calls booked and proposals sent. A redone website is not a result under rule 1.
  3. There is no evidence that the site is blocking any sale. That leaves its effect on the outcome (first paying client by 20 December) speculative, while the outreach has a direct effect (rule 5).
- **Why:** Rule 2 has one exception, "a fix that is blocking a specific sale," and the case doesn't meet it. The reason given is general ("people look you up"), unverified, and tied to no named prospect or sale. Under rules 2 and 5, the Wednesday outreach to 15 new contacts moves this month's outcome more directly than a website redo does, so the current decision stands.
- **What would change my mind:** Evidence that the website is blocking a specific sale. For example, a named prospect who said they won't reply, book a call or sign because of the site, or a documented case of a current prospect going quiet after visiting it. Even then, the change I'd expect is a narrow fix aimed at that blocker, not a full redo that replaces the outreach.
