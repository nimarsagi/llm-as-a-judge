# Rules

Business: one-person AI consultancy. Stage: 0 to 10K a month (average received over the last 3 months: 0).
This month's outcome: first paying client signed by 20 December.

1. Targets are results (discovery calls booked, proposals sent, revenue received), not effort.
2. Before the first paying client, sales comes first: outreach, discovery calls, proposals. Marketing, branding and website work wait until revenue comes in, except a fix that is blocking a specific sale.
3. At most 2 major tasks per weekday.
4. No hires or paid help before revenue has covered the cost for 3 months running.
5. A task earns a slot only if it moves this month's outcome.
